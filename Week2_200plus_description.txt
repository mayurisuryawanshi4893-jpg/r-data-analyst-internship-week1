This Week 2 project focuses on data visualization and insight communication using R and ggplot2. The IBM Telco Customer Churn dataset from Week 1 was continued so that the visualization work could build on a previously cleaned dataset. The analysis uses 7,032 customer records after handling missing TotalCharges values. Key variables include customer churn status, tenure, total charges, monthly charges, and contract type.

Six complementary visualizations were created to communicate different aspects of the data. A bar chart was used to compare the number of customers who churned and those who remained. A histogram was selected to show the distribution of customer tenure and identify where customers are concentrated across their relationship duration. A scatter plot was used to examine the relationship between tenure and total charges, with a fitted trend line to make the overall positive association easier to interpret.

A contract-level bar chart was developed to compare churn rates across month-to-month, one-year, and two-year contracts. This visualization highlights a major difference in churn behaviour, with month-to-month customers showing substantially higher churn than customers on longer contracts. A line chart was used to communicate how average monthly charges vary across tenure. Finally, a box plot compared monthly-charge distributions between churned and non-churned customers, allowing the median and spread of the two groups to be compared.

Each visualization was selected according to the analytical question it was intended to answer. The report includes the R code used to generate the charts, the graph outputs, and a narrative interpretation written for a non-technical audience. Overall, the visual analysis shows that contract type, tenure, and monthly charges are important variables to investigate when understanding customer churn. The project demonstrates how R visualizations can turn raw customer data into clear, actionable insights while maintaining reproducibility through code.
